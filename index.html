Alfred

A private, single-user personal assistant

Alfred is a personal assistant that Rob Brown built for his own use. It runs on Rob's own computer and phone and helps him with his email, calendar, documents, notes and research.

Alfred is not a product or service. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than Rob can sign in to it or use it.

With Rob's permission, Alfred connects to Rob's own Google account to read and organize his Gmail and to view and manage his Google Calendar. How that information is handled is described in the privacy policy.
