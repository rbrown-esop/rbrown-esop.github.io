Privacy policy

Alfred · Last updated November 8, 2026

Who this covers

Alfred is a private assistant built and used by one person, Rob Brown. The only Google account it connects to is Rob's own. It has no other users and collects no information about anyone else's account.

What Alfred accesses

When Rob grants permission through Google's sign-in screen, Alfred can:

  • Gmail: read, search, label and organize Rob's messages, and prepare drafts or send messages that Rob has approved.
  • Google Calendar: view Rob's calendars and create, change or delete events at Rob's request.

How the information is used

Alfred uses this information only to carry out the tasks Rob asks for, such as answering questions about his schedule, summarizing or finding email, and drafting replies. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles for anyone else.

To understand Rob's requests and write its replies, Alfred sends the relevant text to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Anthropic handles that data under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models.

Storage

Alfred runs on Rob's own computer. The sign-in token Google issues, Alfred's conversation history and its notes are stored there and nowhere else. Alfred keeps no copy of Rob's mailbox or calendar; it fetches what it needs when asked.

Sharing

Information from Rob's Google account is not shared with or transferred to anyone, except Anthropic as described above for processing his requests, or where the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Rob can withdraw Alfred's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored token and history from his computer.
